# Public process reference for invoice matching

Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/finance/accounts-payable/accounts-payable-invoice-matching

Accessed: 2026-10-09. Page last updated: 2025-05-15.

Microsoft describes invoice matching as comparing vendor invoices with purchase orders and receipts. Two-way matching compares price; three-way matching also compares received quantities. Matching discrepancies are checked against company-selected tolerances. Prior invoice lines can matter when partially invoicing an order.
