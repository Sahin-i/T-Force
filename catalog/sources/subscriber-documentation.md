# Public process reference for corporate subscribers

Source: https://www.azercell.com/en/personal/support/number-purchase-and-operations/who-may-be-a-subscriber-of-azercell.html

Accessed: 2026-10-09. Relevant sections: commercial legal entities 2.1 and notes 4 and 5.

The published requirements include a legal-entity register extract, authority to act for the entity, and identification of the authorized representative. The tax-registration certificate and bank-details letter are optional in the listed commercial-entity section. Electronically registered entities have electronic extracts without a stamp; information is compared with official-register information before acceptance.

Stamp requirements are conditional: the published note distinguishes a contract signed by the entity's head from one signed by an authorized person under power of attorney. Signature authenticity remains an employee check in this demonstration.

All application documents, identification numbers, registry records and provisioning queues supplied with this catalog are synthetic. This is a scoped fictional procedure, not a complete legal or subscriber eligibility determination.
