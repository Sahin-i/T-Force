# Public process reference for complaint investigation

Source: https://www.azercell.com/en/personal/help/complaint-handling.html

Accessed: 2026-10-09. Relevant section: investigation and solution of received complaints.

Azercell describes recording requests that cannot be resolved immediately, asking for relevant subscriber and complaint details, investigating relevant electronic databases, and extending an investigation when necessary with other people or sources. The published process includes informing the subscriber about investigation and resolution.

The catalog's duplicate-event credit formula, account snapshots, transactions, response letters and simulated resolution queue are explicit fictional demonstration policy. The public page is not evidence that this credit policy is used by Azercell or that Azercell's complaint process is inefficient.
